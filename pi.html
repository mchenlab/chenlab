PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Maorong Chen 陈茂荣

Connecting molecular mechanisms with the behavior of cells and tissues.

Maorong Chen leads the laboratory in the School of Life Sciences at Yunnan University. His work examines Wnt receptor regulation, intracellular signaling dynamics, intestinal stem-cell biology, and colorectal cancer.

Before joining Yunnan University in 2022, he conducted research at Boston Children’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School, where he also served as an Instructor.
