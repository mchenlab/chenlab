OUR CENTRAL QUESTION

How does a signal at the cell surface
change the fate of a tissue?

Our research connects the molecular organization of Wnt signaling with intestinal stem-cell biology and colorectal cancer. By understanding how receptor availability, signaling dynamics, and the cellular environment work together, we aim to uncover new ways to control cell behavior.
